All About Bunnies

Rabbits are small, social mammals known for their long ears, soft fur, and strong hind legs. They are intelligent and curious animals that can form close bonds with humans. Rabbits are often kept as pets and require proper care, attention, and space to live healthy, happy lives.

Rabbit Care

Rabbits need clean living spaces, fresh water, gentle handling, and regular grooming to stay healthy.

Rabbit Diet

A healthy rabbit diet includes hay, leafy greens, fresh water, and limited pellets every day.

Rabbit Behavior

Rabbits are active, curious, and social animals that enjoy safe spaces, toys, and interaction.
